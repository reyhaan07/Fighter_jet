STRIKE WING - PC version
========================

HOW TO PLAY
1. Right-click the ZIP file and choose "Extract All...". Do not run it from inside the ZIP.
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click "Strike Wing.exe".
3. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" then "Run anyway".
   (The game is not signed with a paid certificate; it is safe and fully offline.)
4. Optional: right-click "Strike Wing.exe" > Send to > Desktop (create shortcut).

No installation and no internet needed. Your progress is saved automatically.

CONTROLS
Arrow keys / mouse: fly      Left click: gun      Right click: missile
Mouse wheel or W/S: speed    Shift: afterburner   R/Q: switch weapon
X: flares   V: chaff/defence   T: next target   C: camera   Esc: pause

If it runs slowly: Esc > Settings > Graphics > pick "Low" or "Lite".
